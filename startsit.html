How the call is made

Chance to outscore comes from simulating each player's week 20,000 times using their range of outcomes (built from how players with similar projections actually scored). In 2025 testing, head-to-head calls the model gave about 64% won 64% of the time, and 74% calls won 75%. Kicker matchups are close to coin flips.

Most points starts whoever projects highest. Safe floor favors the better bad-week outcome (useful when you're favored), and Big upside favors the better big-week outcome (when you need a boom game to win). The players are simulated independently, so two players in the same game aren't treated as linked.

Out and Doubtful players aren't listed. Once a game kicks off its projection is locked, and actual points appear after the final whistle.
