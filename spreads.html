How to use this

Fair odds are the break-even price. A 85% line is only worth betting if your sportsbook pays better than the fair odds shown (for example −500 is better than −600). Books usually price alt lines with extra margin, so a safe line is often a bad deal even when it covers.

How the lines are built. The expected margin starts at the Vegas spread and is nudged by the model's disagreement with Vegas. Cover chances come from the actual final margins of historical games with a similar spread, so key numbers like 3 and 7 are priced correctly.

Safe isn't guaranteed. At 85%, about 1 in 7 of these lines will still lose. Parlaying several "safe" lines multiplies the risk: five 85% legs all win only about 44% of the time.
