Reading the table

Proj is expected PPR points. Floor – ceiling is the range the player lands in about 70% of weeks (15th–85th percentile of past outcomes for similar projections); the tick marks the projection.

Matchup grade compares the projection with the same player facing an average defense, with an average team total, at a neutral site. The change is measured against at least a typical fantasy starter's score (16 pts QB, 12 RB/WR, 9 TE), so backups can't earn big grades from tiny swings: A+ is +15% or more, A +8%, B +3%, C roughly neutral, D −3%, F −8% or worse. Hide backups (on by default) shows depth-chart starters plus anyone ranked as a fantasy starter. The rank shows how that defense stacks up against the position (1st = allows the most points above expectation). Early in the season, defense data leans on last year and is pulled toward average.

Team pts is the Vegas-implied total for the player's team. Q = questionable (Out and Doubtful players are left off). New = little NFL history, so the projection leans on what players at that depth-chart spot usually score. Once a game kicks off, its projections are locked and Actual fills in after the final whistle.
